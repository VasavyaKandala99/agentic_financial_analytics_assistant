
TREASURY ANALYTICS KNOWLEDGE BASE

Transaction Volume:
Transaction volume is the total value of payment transactions processed
during a specified period.

Failed Transaction Rate:
Failed transaction rate is the percentage of attempted transactions that
did not complete successfully.

Liquidity Risk:
Liquidity risk is the risk that an organization may not have sufficient
cash or liquid assets to meet its short-term financial obligations.

Foreign Exchange Exposure:
Foreign exchange exposure represents financial risk caused by changes
in currency exchange rates.

Average Transaction Value:
Average transaction value is calculated by dividing total transaction
volume by the number of transactions.

Settlement:
Settlement is the process through which funds related to a transaction
are transferred between participating financial institutions.
